The Coffee Business

My Role

As a hardworking cafe worker, I am responsible for preparing beverages, serving customers, and helping to maintain a clean and comfortable environment in the café. I always strive to provide friendly service and ensure that every customer feels welcomed and satisfied. I believe that a great coffee shop is not only about the quality of the drinks, but also about the positive experience and warm atmosphere created for the community.

Business Concept

The cafe is designed as a calm and aesthetic space inspired by local village life. It blends modern coffee culture with traditional warmth, creating a unique identity in Perlis.

Contribution to Community

This business supports the local economy and provides a social space where people can relax, work, and connect. It also promotes local culture through its environment and menu.

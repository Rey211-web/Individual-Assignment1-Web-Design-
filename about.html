Background

My name is Mohammad Usamah bin Shaiful Hizam. I was born on 14 June 1998 and I am currently 28 years old. I am married and based in Alor Setar, Kedah. As a cafe worker, I take pride in providing good service and creating a welcoming environment for customers. I am a dedicated and hardworking individual who values discipline, responsibility, and positive customer relationships. Working in the coffee industry has helped me improve my communication skills, teamwork, and ability to handle customers professionally. Through my work, I aim not only to serve quality coffee and beverages but also to create a comfortable and enjoyable experience for the community.

Journey

My journey began in the field of computing at GIATMARA, where I developed technical knowledge and discipline. I later worked as a laptop technician, a role that strengthened my problem-solving mindset and attention to detail.

However, my true passion was always rooted in entrepreneurship. I wanted to build something meaningful not just a business, but a place where people feel at home. This vision led me to establish my own coffee shop in Kampung Wai.

Experience

From 2020 to 2022, I worked at PC Depot, handling laptop repairs and maintenance. This experience taught me patience, customer service, and precision skills that I now apply in managing my café.

Skills

• Strong communication and customer engagement

• Technical troubleshooting and adaptability

• Small business management and creativity
